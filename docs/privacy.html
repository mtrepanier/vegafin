VegaFin Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026

VegaFin is a client application for Jellyfin, an open-source media server. VegaFin does not operate any server, backend, or cloud service of its own. This page explains what information VegaFin handles and where it goes.

What VegaFin sends, and to whom

When you add a server in VegaFin, the server address and the sign-in credentials you enter (username/password, or an access token) are sent directly from your device to the Jellyfin server you specify - a server you or someone you trust runs and controls. VegaFin's developer does not operate that server, and does not receive, see, or have access to your credentials, your library, or your watch activity.

What VegaFin stores on your device

Locally, on your own device only, VegaFin stores:

  • The Jellyfin server address(es) you've added and the access token(s) issued to you by that server (used to keep you signed in without re-entering your password).
  • App preferences, such as your selected language and playback settings.

None of this is transmitted anywhere other than to the Jellyfin server it belongs to. Removing a server or user from VegaFin removes this data from your device.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

VegaFin does not include analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, or advertising software of any kind, and does not share any information with third parties.

Your Jellyfin server's own practices

The media library, artwork, and playback data you see in VegaFin come from the Jellyfin server you connect to. That server is operated independently - by you, or by whoever you obtained access from - and this policy does not cover its practices. Check with that server's operator for how it handles your data.

Children's privacy

VegaFin is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via VegaFin's GitHub issue tracker.
